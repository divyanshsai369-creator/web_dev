About Me

Designing with
purpose and
precision.

I'm a product designer with 8 years of experience crafting digital experiences across SaaS, fintech, and consumer products. I believe great design is invisible — it solves problems so elegantly that the solution feels inevitable.

My work spans the full product lifecycle: from user research and strategy through to interaction design, design systems, and front-end implementation. I collaborate closely with engineers and stakeholders to ship products that are both beautiful and functional.

When I'm not designing, I write about design systems, speak at conferences, and mentor junior designers breaking into the industry.
